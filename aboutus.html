About Me

Reena Vollala

Through this assignment, I learned how Management Information Systems help businesses organize information, improve efficiency, and make decisions. I also learned how artificial intelligence can support learning and productivity when it is used responsibly. Comparing correct and incorrect AI usage showed me why transparency and verifying information matter. Researching the WannaCry attack helped me understand how malware can disrupt organizations and why security updates are important. Building this website gave me practice with HTML tables, hyperlinks, images, navigation, and different types of lists. Overall, I saw how MIS, AI, and cybersecurity work together in modern organizations.
