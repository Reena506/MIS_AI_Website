Correct AI Usage

My Reasoning

AI can be helpful in college when students use it to clarify difficult concepts and practice new skills. It can provide examples, explain confusing material, and suggest improvements to writing. Students should check the information it produces and follow their instructors’ rules about acceptable assistance. The final work should show what the student understands rather than simply copying an AI response.

AI Critique of the Draft

I agree with your point that AI should help students learn rather than replace their effort. Your examples of explanations and writing suggestions are practical, and mentioning course rules makes your argument stronger. One area to improve is specificity: you could explain how a student might use AI to practice SQL without copying a completed homework answer. I would also add a reminder to disclose AI assistance and protect private information when using these tools.

Benefits of AI

  1. Explains difficult topics in different ways
  2. Supports brainstorming and practice

Who Benefits?

  1. Students who need extra practice
  2. Employees who analyze large amounts of information

How AI Tools Used

  • Creating sample quiz questions
  • Revising writing when permitted and disclosed

Incorrect AI Usage

Misrepresenting authorship
Submitting AI-generated work as independently written when that is not allowed.

Inventing references
Using citations without checking whether the referenced sources exist.

Sharing confidential information
Uploading personal or workplace data without authorization.

I believe AI is useful for learning, but honesty matters more than convenience. Students should verify AI-generated claims before using them. Presenting generated answers as original work can violate academic policies. Responsible use requires transparency and personal judgment.
