Malware and Cyber Threats

The WannaCry Ransomware Attack

On May 12, 2017, the WannaCry ransomware attack began spreading across computers in many countries. The malware encrypted files and demanded payment, disrupting organizations including parts of the United Kingdom's National Health Service. The event demonstrated how unpatched systems can expose critical services to widespread cyberattacks.

Types of Malware

  2. Trojan horses
